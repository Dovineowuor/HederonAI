## Slide 4: The Solution (Project Summary) (b)
**The Coordinated AI Swarm**
- **Actionable Execution:** Users specify a high-level goal, and the CEO Agent automatically creates a project plan, delegating to specialized agents.
- **Brainstorming Challenges:** A dedicated lateral-thinking mode where specialized agents (Researcher, Analyst, Designer, Creator) ideate and create downloadable, IPFS-backed deliverables (e.g. Codebases, PDFs).
- **Hedera Powered:** Trustless execution environments.
    - *HCS (Consensus Service):* Immutable logging of what the AI decided to do and why.
    - *HTS (Token Service):* Simulated microtransactions validating an agent-to-agent token economy.

## Slide 6: Hedera Integration Unpacked (b)
*Highlighting exactly why we built on Hedera.*
- **Unrivaled Transparency:** We map AI outputs directly into immutable HCS topics.
- **Micro-economies at scale:** HTS facilitates near-zero cost, instant royalty and resource payments between AI agents.
- **Decentralized Permanence:** IPFS integration guarantees that agent-generated "Files" and "Codebases" exist independently of Web2 servers.
